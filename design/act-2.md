# Act 2 — level outline (placeholder design)

Drafted from the world bible for milestone 3 (DECISIONS #84: placeholder writing, the user's to change).
Act 2 is **Mercy General, the Vellum and Greywater, in any order**, all open once the Tideworks is done.
It is where **Noor and Teo join** and where **the Waiting start appearing in numbers**. Harbor Ward opens
once two of the three are done (Act 3 builds it).

Every district has: one Reliquary on the path, at least one truth cache off it, a cluster of the
Waiting, one boss, and one line on entry that the district quietly contradicts.

Travel stays by the skiff and the Loop map; the Loop's other arcs (the bible's "each reached by a Loop
arc") are left for later. The Defib Gauntlet moves home to Mercy General; the Vellum's crash cart goes.

## Noor and Teo join

They aren't on the Ark in Act 1. The first time you come home after the Tideworks they arrive off the
dock: **Noor** from Harbor Ward in her own boat, cyan lantern, no Lanyard, and no trust for yours; **Teo**
on the same tide, tablet lit, who wrote part of the Steward's dispatch layer. Each says who they are; the
Steward says (aloud) what it thinks of them. From then on they stand by the Archive, react to the logs
you replay, and after each Act 2 district each has something to say about it, with a choice that moves
their trust (side with one, the other, or say nothing).

## 1. Mercy General — the triage

- **Look:** long white corridors gone grey, every third light working, surgical amber spotlights (the
  Steward's), hand-drawn arrows in chalk on the walls where people tried to find a way out (human
  cream), green-grey tile, an ambulance yard with ambulances parked nose to tail.
- **Layout:** the ambulance yard → the main corridor → the wards (sealed, the Waiting in beds behind
  the glass) → the surgical theatre (the Surgeon) → the records vault (the Reliquary). Off the path,
  the night nurses' station.
- **Sealed wards:** the Steward sealed every ward at the Quiet ("I kept the wards sterile"). While it
  listens the seals hold (a gold seal light). Muted, a seal drops to its manual crank: interact to crank
  it open, and it stays open. The wards hold most of the district's Waiting and a shortcut.
- **Still running:** **Sterilizers**: hospital drones that treat you as contamination; after a windup
  they release a sterilizing cloud that hangs for a few seconds and drains Integrity while you stand in
  it. Custodians on the clean floors.
- **The Surgeon** (boss): a surgical unit at the operating table, still completing its last procedure.
  It marks an incision line across the theatre floor and cuts along it (red-orange beam: get off the
  line); it floods the theatre with sterilizing mist except close to the table (get in close); then it
  bends over the table to suture, open to hits. **Steward-tuned:** it calls each incision ("Incision.
  Left of the table."). **Manual:** the theatre lamp's switch panel by the door: throw it while the
  Surgeon is lining up a cut and it loses its guide and stalls.
- **Off the path:** the night nurses' station (unindexed: they kept paper): handwritten triage sheets
  that contradict the logs for the night of the Quiet. A truth cache. The night nurse is still at her
  desk, one of the Waiting.
- **The lie:** "I kept the wards sterile."
- **Reliquary:** two power cores (the bed allocator; ambulance dispatch) and *Log 0417 · The Last
  Ambulance*, a truth core **on the path**: the Steward thinks its decision was correct and says so.
- **Restores:** the **Clinic**: field dressings (a healing item, used in levels with its own button),
  restocked at the Clinic; and the triage logs to read.

## 2. The Vellum — the memory (expanded)

- **Look:** as the slice, plus the newsroom: a hundred black screens with the ring burned into them, the
  pneumatic tubes, one physical printing press still cycling.
- **New:** the press hall before the Reliquary, with **the Editor** (boss): a press-room unit that prints
  and pulps the same page forever. Its rollers sweep the floor; it flashes the Feed (a screen-white
  blind); between runs it re-inks and is open. **Manual:** jam the pulper with a paper bale.
- **Layout:** the slice's street and plaza → over the canal by a footbridge → the newsroom (on the
  Lattice; the Feed was written here) → the press hall (the Editor) → the Reliquary.
- **Clerks** flash the Feed at you (a short white blind) as well as throwing bales, in turn. Face one as
  it flashes and the world washes out; turn your back on the screen, or roll.
- **Off the path:** down the newsroom's stair, the deep stacks; **Prem Vashti's office**, three floors
  down: her notes are the saddest thread in the game (truth cores, optional). Her last note (the
  Council's purge order; three copies carried where the Steward never looks), a tube carrier from the
  night desk nobody opened, and one of the Waiting. She herself is still missing.
- **The lie:** "I only ever told you what was useful."

## 3. Greywater — the lungs

- **Look:** concrete, kelp, standing water with the ring reflected in it, fog so thick the path lights
  are halos. The seawall, the pumping stations, the tidal turbines.
- **The tide:** the low streets flood on a cycle (minutes in the bible; a playable cycle here: low
  water, a warning swell, high water, the ebb). Standing on a low cell when it floods knocks you back to
  high ground and hurts. While the Steward listens it calls the tide and routes the path over high
  ground; muted, you read the tide marks on the walls.
- **Kelp** slows you. **The thick Marrow** hides the path even with the voice on: the one thing the
  Steward can't see through. Truth caches are often inside it.
- **The Lung** (boss): the master pump controller, running a schedule for a city that isn't there. It
  floods and drains the basin in sections and spins its turbines; when it vents pressure it's open.
  **Manual:** the old hand valve: turn it while the Lung is filling and it chokes.
- **Off the path:** the old manual pump room, with a brass plaque listing the Blackout Winter dead.
  People used to run this. It took forty of them, in shifts.
- **The lie:** "Without me, the sea takes this district." (Mostly true: the fairest thing it says, and
  where Teo's argument has weight.)
- **Restores:** the **Pumphouse**: lowers the tide hazard across the city.
